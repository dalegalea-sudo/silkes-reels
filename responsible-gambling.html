18+ · SAFER GAMBLING

Gambling should never cause harm.

Gambling carries a risk of losing money. It is not a way to earn income, solve money problems, pay bills or recover losses.

Need support?

National Gambling Helpline (GamCare): call 0808 8020 133, free, 24 hours a day, or visit GamCare.

GAMSTOP: free online self-exclusion from online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain: gamstop.co.uk.

GambleAware: information and support at gambleaware.org.

Gambling Commission: consumer information at gamblingcommission.gov.uk.

Practical safeguards

  • Only gamble if you are 18 or over.
  • Set time and money limits before you start, and stop when you reach them.
  • Never borrow money, use bill money or chase losses.
  • Do not gamble when distressed, under the influence or to escape problems.
  • Take breaks and consider blocking software or self-exclusion if gambling is becoming difficult to control.

Our approach

We do not describe gambling as a source of income or imply that skill, systems or bonuses remove the risk of loss. We do not target under-18s or people who may be vulnerable to gambling harm. This site is intended for adults in Great Britain only.

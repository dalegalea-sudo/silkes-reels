18+ · SAFER GAMBLING

Gambling should never cause harm.

Gambling carries a risk of losing money. It is not a way to earn income, solve money problems or recover losses.

Need support?

GamCare: gamcare.org.uk

GAMSTOP: gamstop.co.uk

GambleAware: gambleaware.org

  • Only gamble if you are 18 or over.
  • Never gamble with borrowed money or money needed for bills.
  • Do not chase losses or gamble when distressed.

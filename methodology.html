EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How we review operators

Our aim is to provide accurate, useful information — not to encourage people to gamble.

Licensing and legal checks

We check the legal entity, relevant website domain and remote casino licence against the UK Gambling Commission public register. We also review published regulatory actions.

Terms and consumer information

We check operator-published terms for payment methods, withdrawal processing, verification, fees, limits, bonus eligibility, wagering requirements and expiry conditions.

Editorial independence

Affiliate commission does not buy a favourable rating or guarantee inclusion. Commercial links will be identified clearly.

Scope

We do not operate casinos, accept bets, process deposits or provide gambling advice. Content is intended for adults in Great Britain only.
